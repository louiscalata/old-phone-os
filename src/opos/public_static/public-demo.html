Old Phone OS Interface

A quieter phone interface that pauses less essential work to keep calls and messages going.

Interactive edition · Simulation only
View source ↗Read the story ↗

Hackathon prototype. This public demo simulates the battery, calls and messages; it is not a phone operating system and has not run on a physical phone.

  1. 01
    Open ?demo=1 controlsStarts unlocked at noon: 60% charge × 60% health is about 36% usable. Open the top battery bar for the 4 PM goal and time-left estimate.
  2. 02
    Undo a soft pause firstAt 60%, click Undo beside maps in battery details. Maps resumes and the estimate gets shorter. Close the panel before changing the battery.
  3. 03
    Try the low-battery floorSet Battery to 20%. Non-essential tasks pause. Enter “Why are maps paused?” and Ask (Show helper if hidden): battery rules explain the pauses while calls and messages stay allowed.
  4. 04
    Try charging and heatTick Charging: tasks can resume. Set Temp to 45°C: only essentials run, even charging. Restore Temp to 30°C; at 0% Battery, the call reserve still pauses other tasks.
  5. 05
    See the saving steps and Why?Restore Battery to 60% and untick Charging. Plan proposes dim screen, short screen timeout, then batch sync; it does not run them. Why? explains the pauses from battery rules. Muse Glimmer is off here.

Your own temporary phone. Resets after 15 minutes away or when the demo is busy.
